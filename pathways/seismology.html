Session brief

Computational Seismology

The session combines computational seismology with hydro-geological modelling, and frames the engineering problem as a budget: the time and resources available between detection of an earthquake and the arrival of damaging ground motion.

The session brief is published; the interactive apparatus and worksheets for this pathway are still being prepared, so this page currently records the session and its place in the programme rather than a finished instrument.

Read the brief file Back to the catalogue

The hydro-geological half of the session returns to the water pathway: the same subsurface transport that governs contaminant movement also governs how ground motion and groundwater respond.
