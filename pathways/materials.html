Materials Modelling

Equation of state and lattice dynamics, supported by a presentation on how molecular dynamics, density functional theory and machine learning are used to design materials that remove contaminants from water.

The session

Equation of State & Lattice Dynamics

The session treats a solid as a lattice of atoms whose response to temperature and pressure is written as an equation of state, and whose collective vibrations are described as lattice dynamics. The same logic — write the physics, then compute the consequence — is what the accompanying presentation applies to water treatment.

Presentation

Clean Water Through Materials Modelling

Eighteen slides prepared for World Rivers Day 2026 under the theme clean rivers and sustainable livelihoods. The deck walks from the sources and health effects of water contamination, through the treatment steps used at Barekese, to the three computational methods the programme teaches.

Two of the slides are working applications rather than static figures. The factor analysis moves temperature, pressure, pH and competing-ion concentration and shows how the predicted capture efficiency responds. The machine-learning slide trains a small network on simulated material samples and lets you watch the decision boundary form.
