Pathway · Sessions 01–10

Water & Wastewater Engineering

Ten bench-scale sessions covering the physical, chemical and hydraulic analysis of drinking water and wastewater — the foundation series of the laboratory.

10 sessionsBench methodsApparatus in preparation

Session briefs

The ten water sessions

The series moves from what a sample contains, through the treatment train that removes it, to the hydraulics that deliver it. The titles below are the published session briefs; each line records the measurement that session is built around. Interactive apparatus for this pathway is still being built.

  1. 01

    Turbidity & Apparent Colour

    Optical clarity of a water sample, measured as turbidity alongside apparent colour.

    Brief
  2. 02

    pH, Acidity & Alkalinity

    The acid–base balance of a sample: free and total acidity, and alkalinity to a chosen endpoint.

    Brief
  3. 03

    Total Hardness by EDTA Titration

    Calcium and magnesium content determined by complexometric titration with EDTA.

    Brief
  4. 04

    Jar Test: Coagulation & Flocculation

    Coagulant dosing, rapid mixing and flocculation, compared across jars.

    Brief
  5. 05

    Sedimentation: Type I Settling

    Discrete particle settling in a sedimentation basin, and the overflow rate that governs removal.

    Brief
  6. 06

    Rapid Sand Filtration

    Granular media filtration: media depth, rate and head loss across the bed.

    Brief
  7. 07

    Breakpoint Chlorination

    Chlorine demand and the breakpoint, read from a dose–residual curve.

    Brief
  8. 08

    Dissolved Oxygen & BOD₅

    Oxygen status of a sample and its five-day biochemical oxygen demand.

    Brief
  9. 09

    Chemical Oxygen Demand

    Oxidisable matter measured by dichromate digestion.

    Brief
  10. 10

    Head Loss in Water Distribution Pipes

    Friction losses in distribution pipes and their effect on network pressure.

    Brief
