Session brief

Smart Renewable Energy

The session addresses solar photovoltaic efficiency — the fraction of incident solar energy a panel converts into electrical power — together with the inverter control that turns the panel's direct-current output into a usable alternating supply.

The session brief is published; the interactive apparatus and worksheets for this pathway are still being prepared, so this page currently records the session and its place in the programme rather than a finished instrument.

Read the brief file Back to the catalogue

Renewable energy sits alongside the modelling pathways: the same computational methods used for materials and quantum devices apply to photovoltaic conversion and power conditioning.
